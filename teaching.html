Supervising Thesis

I supervise thesis projects across three active research areas. Students may choose from the following focus types depending on their interests and goals.

Research Projects

∑ AlgeSPACE

Algebra Scaffolding with Pedagogical Agents and Concrete Examples

A research project focused on designing and studying an adaptive mathematics tutoring environment for algebra learning. The system combines real-world concrete examples, support for strategic flexibility in problem solving, personalized pedagogical agents, and self-regulated learning scaffolds such as goal setting and motivational prompts.

🐦 SMILE

Science Misconception-Focused Immersive Learning Environment

A research project focused on designing and studying an immersive science learning environment that helps students understand emergent phenomena and address common misconceptions in complex systems. The environment combines interactive simulation, LLM-based agents, and opportunities for generative learning activities (e.g., making predictions) as well as metacognitive monitoring (e.g., confidence-check reflections).

🌍 AGENTIC

Agency-Supportive GEN-AI Teaching Agents for Individualized Coaching

A research project focused on designing and studying Generative AI-based Teaching Agents that not only provide individualized support but also strengthen student and teacher agency. Through a 3D simulator, the agents can support role-based exploration and coach students' self-regulated learning through goal setting, planning, monitoring, and reflection. The project also aims to support data-informed teaching and AI technology integration.

Thesis Focus Types

  • 🔬 Experiment-focused — You’d design instructional principles or activities using an existing learning environment and conduct an experiment, potentially using eye-tracking, to test their effectiveness and your research questions.
  • 📊 Data analysis-focused — You’d use an existing dataset, such as log data, video data, survey data, interview data, learning performance data, or multimodal data, to answer your own research questions.
  • 🎨 Design-focused — You’d engage in iterative (co-)design research with practitioners, community members, or students to produce digital artifacts, which would be followed by user testing or evaluations.
  • 📚 Systematic Literature Review — You’d systematically search, select, review, and synthesize existing research on a specific topic to identify key findings, trends, gaps, and future research directions.
