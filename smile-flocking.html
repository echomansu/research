SMILE Program · Project

Flocking Simulator 🐑

Walk into a flock. Watch a pattern nobody is in charge of.

In this project, learners control a virtual dog in an open world to explore animal groups exhibiting collective behavior, such as sheep flocks, duck flocks, and bat swarms, as analogies for emergent processes in complex systems. The environment combines interactive simulation, an LLM-based tutor, prediction activities, and confidence-check reflections.

Research Questions

  1. How does learner engagement within an open-world, self-paced, LLM-supported learning environment relate to learning outcomes?
  2. How do parameter control and questioning agency affect learners' understanding of emergent phenomena in complex systems?
  3. How can conceptual frameworks such as PAIR-C (Patterns, Agents, Interactions, Relations, and Causality) be integrated into the design of interactive simulation-based learning environments?

Inside the Environment

Three lessons of increasing conceptual difficulty, each anchored in a different animal group, with an LLM-based Butterfly Tutor (powered by GPT-4o) available throughout for adaptive, PAIR-C grounded support.

The Driving Question

Free Exploration

Learners roam the open world and approach animal groups in any order.

🐑 Lesson 1 · Sheep

Basic level: emergent Patterns and Agents. "Show Interactions" reveals the hidden network.

🐑 Lesson 1 · Talking It Through

The tutor answers in the language of emergence, then returns a question.

🦆 Lesson 2 · Ducks

Intermediate level: the three Boids rules, covering Interactions and Relations.

🦆 Lesson 2 · Prediction Phase

A commitment before touching any control.

🦆 Lesson 2 · Manipulation Phase

Alignment, cohesion, and separation sliders test the prediction.

🦇 Lesson 3 · Bats

Complex level: Causality. Does the pattern hold when speed changes?

🦇 Lesson 3 · A Fourth Parameter

Speed shifts every bat at once: a net effect, not a chain reaction.

🦋 Tutor · On Demand

PAIR-C grounded guidance, contextualized by regional metadata.

🦋 Tutor · Non-Intrusive

A blinking prompt offers help without demanding it.

🦋 Tutor · Corrective Feedback

Elaborated feedback on open-ended answers, including transfer to systems like Earth's climate.
